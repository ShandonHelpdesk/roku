‌Privacy Policy

Shandon Baptist Church Roku Channel · Effective date: July 29, 2026


This Privacy Policy explains how Shandon Baptist Church ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our Roku streaming channel (the "App"), which provides access to our live and recorded worship services. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.


Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to watch our content. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information through the App. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking within the App.


Information Processed by Third Parties

The App runs on Roku devices and relies on third-party services to deliver content. These providers may process limited technical information (such as device type, IP address, and general usage data) as a normal part of delivering streaming video. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies, including:

Roku (the device platform) — see Roku's Privacy Policy

Resi (our live-streaming provider)

GitHub (which hosts the App's images and configuration files)

We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.


Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
